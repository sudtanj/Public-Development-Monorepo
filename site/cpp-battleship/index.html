Battleship (C++)

A browser version of cpp-battleship/main.cpp, a console game. Clicking a cell types its column and row into the game, and the panel on the right shows the program's output. The game logic is a line-by-line model of the C++ code, and CI checks the real program by compiling it and playing a full game.

Quirks of the original (kept on purpose)


    
  
  • COM never shoots back, so you can't lose. The game ends after you destroy 3 ships.
    • 
  
  • "Press 1 to start" isn't read, so the first number you type is already the column.
    • 
  
  • Hitting a ship sinks only the hit cell and the cells to its right. The loop meant to clear the left side starts on
    a cell that was just cleared, so it never runs. The leftover part counts as another ship if you hit it.
    • 
  
  • The sinking loop doesn't stop at the edge of the board. A ship ending in column 10 also sinks a ship that starts in
    column 1 of the next row.
    • 
  
  • Shooting your own ships or the same cell twice is allowed and does nothing.
